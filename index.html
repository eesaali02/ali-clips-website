Ali Clips Studio is a private home studio at 844 Preston Manor Dr, Mississauga, ON L5V 2L6. Unit details are sent once a booking is confirmed.

Bookings are made through Booksy. Any "Book Now" button on this page links to the live schedule.

Open Sunday, Monday, and Wednesday through Saturday, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Closed Tuesdays.

Appointments only. As a single-chair studio, walk-ins cannot be accommodated. Booking ahead on Booksy is required.

Cash and e-transfer are accepted. Questions before an appointment can be sent by phone or Instagram DM.
